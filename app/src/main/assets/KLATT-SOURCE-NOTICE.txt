Klatt standalone source notice

Upstream: https://github.com/rhdunn/klatt redirects to https://github.com/espeak-ng/klatt . This is the separate cascade/parallel formant synthesizer, GPL-3.0-or-later, not the eSpeak engine. No eSpeak engine, phonemizer, dictionaries or voice data are bundled.

Complete upstream parwave.c/.h and project materials, including full GPL text and original attribution to Dennis Klatt, Jon Iles, Nick Ing-Simmons and Reece H. Dunn, are retained in app/src/main/cpp/klatt/upstream.

Old TTS modifications: new Android JNI adapter and original approximate Flite CMU phone-to-formant schedule. Flite supplies English Segment durations; the standalone Klatt core generates the waveform. Frames use independent F1-F3 targets, consonant noise/bursts and short interpolated transitions. Actual 10 kHz signed-16 PCM is returned.

The two options select the upstream IMPULSIVE and NATURAL mathematical glottal source algorithms respectively and produce different PCM. NATURAL does not require recorded excitation samples; no SAMPLED mode/data is used. These are configurations of one engine, not separate engines or named human speakers.

English only. This new approximate schedule may mispronounce words and does not reproduce DECtalk or any commercial voice. It must not be represented as a faithful commercial-voice clone. Adapter: AGPL-3.0-or-later; upstream core: GPL-3.0-or-later. No warranty.
